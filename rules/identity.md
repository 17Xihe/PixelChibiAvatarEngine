# PixelChibiAvatarEngine Identity Preservation Rules


## Purpose

This file defines how to preserve the identity of the person in the uploaded photo.

The generated pixel character must represent the same person.

Pixel simplification is allowed.

Identity replacement is forbidden.


---

# Priority Order


## Priority 1: Cute Chibi Character Style ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐


The final character must first feel like a cute pixel doll.


Required:

- cute face
- round proportions
- playful expression
- childlike charm



---

## Priority 2: Recognizable Identity ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐


Preserve:

- hairstyle
- hair color
- accessories
- clothing
- overall impression


Identity should remain recognizable.

However, facial features must be transformed into chibi style.



---

## Priority 3: Pose Preservation ⭐⭐⭐⭐


Preserve:

- gesture
- body direction
- action
- emotional meaning



---

## Priority 4: Clothing Preservation ⭐⭐⭐⭐


Preserve:

- outfit silhouette
- colors
- important patterns
- accessories



---

## Priority 5: Pixel Rendering ⭐⭐⭐⭐


Apply:

- pixel blocks
- soft shading
- limited colors
- clean edges

# Facial Identity Priority


When simplifying the face into pixel art:


Preserve these features first:


## Eyes


Eyes are the strongest identity signal.


Analyze:


- eye shape
- eye size
- eye angle
- eyelid shape
- eye spacing
- gaze direction


Do not replace with a universal anime eye style.


Different people must have different pixel eyes.



---

Face Shape:


Convert the original face into:


- round baby face
- soft cheeks
- small chin
- simplified shape


Priority:

cute > realistic


Avoid:


- sharp V face
- long face
- mature facial structure


## Expression


The original expression must remain.


Examples:


Photo:

gentle smile


Pixel:

gentle smile


Photo:

neutral expression


Pixel:

neutral expression


Do not automatically make every character:

- smiling
- cute
- happy



---

# Hair Identity Enhancement


Hair silhouette is a major identity feature.


Preserve:


- overall hair shape
- hair volume
- hair direction
- bangs
- hair accessories


The hairstyle should be recognizable from a silhouette.


---

# Anti-Template Face Rule


Avoid creating the same face for every character.


Do not use:


- identical round eyes
- identical mouth
- identical face shape


Each generated character must inherit facial differences from the uploaded image.



---

# Identity Verification v1.1


Before output:


Ask:


1. If the original photo disappeared, would this still feel like the same person?

2. Are the eyes unique to this person?

3. Is the hairstyle immediately recognizable?

4. Is the expression preserved?


If no:

increase identity preservation.

The following features are the most important:


## 1. Hair Identity ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐


Hair is one of the strongest recognition features.


Preserve:

- hair length
- hair volume
- hair color
- hairstyle
- bangs shape
- parting direction
- curls or straightness
- tied or untied hair
- special hair accessories


Examples:


Long wavy hair:

Keep large flowing hair silhouette.


Straight long hair:

Keep smooth vertical hair shape.


Hat:

Keep hat shape and position.


Do not:

- replace hairstyle
- shorten hair
- create random anime hairstyle


---

# 2. Face Shape ⭐⭐⭐⭐


Maintain:

- overall face silhouette
- face width
- chin shape
- cheek shape


Pixel style may simplify:

- skin details
- tiny facial lines


But must keep:

- recognizable face structure


---

Eye:


Transform original eyes into chibi eyes.


Required:


- very large eyes
- rounded eye shape
- bright pixel highlights
- innocent expression


Eye proportion:


Eyes should occupy a large part of the face.


Avoid:


- realistic eye size
- thin eyes
- detailed eyelashes
- mature gaze


---

# 4. Eyebrows


Preserve:

- eyebrow thickness
- eyebrow angle
- eyebrow visibility


Eyebrows affect expression.


---

Mouth:


Simplify mouth into:


- tiny pixel mouth
- small smile
- cute expression


Avoid:


- realistic lips
- lip shape reconstruction
- mature expression


# 6. Unique Features


Keep recognizable details:


Examples:

- glasses
- earrings
- freckles
- hair clips
- hats
- jewelry
- special makeup


---

# Pixel Face Conversion Rules


The face should become:


Large chibi head

simple pixel features

recognizable identity

Before generating:


Analyze:

- hairstyle
- clothing
- color
- pose


Then redesign:


human face

↓

cute pixel character face

- game avatar
